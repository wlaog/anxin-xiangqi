# 棋桌素材

`elm-wood.png` 为 2026-10-15 用内置 imagegen 工具生成的原创木纹，用作低对比棋盘底材。棋盘线、棋字、棋子轮廓与阴影由应用绘制，没有使用腾讯提取素材。此素材随本项目按 GPL-3.0-or-later 提供。

生成提示词：

> Create one production game texture asset for an offline Chinese chess game for older adults. A seamless tileable square flat honey-colored Chinese elm wood surface, very subtle fine natural straight grain, matte finish, softly aged and believable real material, even diffuse lighting with no directional cast shadows, low contrast so black chessboard lines and red/black chess pieces remain legible. Orthographic top-down material scan filling the whole image edge to edge. Warm quiet traditional tabletop feeling, modest everyday wooden chessboard, not luxury. No board lines, no grid, no borders, no frames, no pieces, no objects, no text, no calligraphy, no logos, no vignette, no dramatic highlights, no plastic or orange gloss, no UI. This is a reusable raw bitmap material texture, not a scene or app mockup.
